02Privacy Notice

For the website and the Help screen. Intended to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Summary

Sarus Operation Planner runs on your own computer. No Sarus server receives your flight data, telemetry, logs, missions or parameters; they stay where they are unless you decide to send them to someone.

What the Software stores on your computer

Settings, telemetry logs, logs downloaded from the flight controller, missions, parameter files and cached map images are kept in Documents\Sarus Operation Planner. A diagnostic log is written to ProgramData\Sarus Operation Planner. Because the logs record the aircraft's GPS position against time, they show where and when you flew, so think before sharing them.

Internet connections the Software makes

When your computer is online, the Software connects to the services below. Each service receives your computer's IP address and the request itself. Sarus does not receive this information.

Your choices

You can work entirely offline: flying, setup over USB and planning with maps cached earlier all work without an internet connection. Usage statistics stay off unless you switch them on, and optional plugins stay disabled unless you enable them. Deleting the folders named above removes your local data at any time.

Support requests

If you send logs or files to Sarus for support, we use them only to resolve your request and delete them after [period]. [Grievance officer / contact: name, email].

Changes

We will publish any change to this notice with the release notes of the version that introduces it.

Notes for counsel
  • The table above reflects the source code of version 1.0.0 (checked on 4 October 2026). Sarus itself currently collects nothing. Personal data handling by Sarus arises only from support requests and from any future Sarus server (for example a Sarus firmware server).
  • Map tiles are fetched directly from third-party providers chosen by the user, as in Mission Planner. Their terms of service may restrict commercial use; review before selling the Software with aircraft.
  • Flight logs may be personal data of the operator, and may be sensitive for defence customers. Consider guidance for customers on log handling.
  • If usage statistics are ever turned on by default, consent under DPDPA would be needed first.

03Safety Disclaimer

Shown in the manual, on the website and on first start.

Unmanned aircraft can cause serious injury, death and damage to property. Sarus Operation Planner helps you configure and monitor an aircraft. It cannot make an aircraft safe, airworthy or legal to fly.

The person operating the aircraft is responsible for every flight, automatic modes included. Keep the radio control transmitter in your hands and be ready to take over at any moment.

Parameters can crash an aircraft. This Software lets you set values outside the recommended ranges and, after a warning, change parameters marked read-only. It will warn you; it will not stop you. Save a backup before you change anything, and test every change on the ground first. Whenever you calibrate, test motors, install firmware or change parameters, the propellers must be off.

Telemetry, maps, elevation, airspace and traffic information can arrive late, be incomplete or be plain wrong. Never rely on them alone to keep clear of terrain, obstacles, people or other aircraft. Radio links fail too, which is why failsafe actions for loss of radio control, telemetry and GPS, and for a low battery, have to be set up and tested.

Register and fly as the Drone Rules, 2021 (as amended), the DGCA and the Digital Sky platform require, including airspace zones, permissions, pilot certification and the Unique Identification Number. Outside India, the law of the country where you fly applies.

Sarus tested this version in ArduPilot's simulator. Before operational use, test it with your own aircraft, flight controller and radios, first on the bench and then in a controlled first flight.

Notes for counsel
  • Point 2 describes deliberate Sarus features (non-blocking range warnings, editable read-only parameters). The disclaimer should make clear that these are the operator's choice.
  • Consider whether a "click to accept" on first start is wanted. Technically it can be added without restricting GPL rights.
  • Product-liability exposure is mainly tied to aircraft that Sarus sells with this software. Consider aligning this text with the aircraft's sale contract and manual.
